1. About this policy

DapperDriver, Inc. ("DapperDriver", "we", "us", "our") operates the DapperDriver mobile application and the website at dapperdriver.com (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect from shoppers, boutique partners, and DapperDriver couriers, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please do not use the Service.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to deliver boutique fashion to your door, run our business, and keep your account secure. The information we collect falls into the following categories:

Account information

  • Name (first and last)
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth and gender (shoppers — used to confirm eligibility and for aggregate demographic insights)
  • Password (stored as a one-way hash — we never see your actual password)
  • Profile photo, if you upload one
  • For boutique partners: business name, business address, tax ID, and Stripe Connect account ID
  • For DapperDrivers: vehicle information, driver's license number (verified through our identity provider), and Stripe Connect account ID

Order and delivery information

  • Delivery address(es)
  • Items you've added to wishlists, items you've ordered, and order history
  • Style preferences and sizes (only if you choose to share them with us to improve recommendations)
  • Communications with boutiques or DapperDrivers about an active order

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. When you add a card, that information is sent directly from your device to Stripe — we never see or store your full card number, CVV, or expiration date. Stripe returns to us a reusable token (not your card number) that we store to charge your authorized card for orders, to let you add a tip after your order is delivered, and to let you reorder without re-entering your card. Saved-card charges are only ever made for tips or orders you initiate, and you can ask us to remove a saved payment method at any time. Stripe is PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. See Stripe's Privacy Policy for how they handle your data.

Precise location

The DapperDriver app may request access to your device's precise location for two narrow purposes: (1) to confirm your delivery address at checkout, and (2) so a DapperDriver can locate your door during an active delivery. We do not collect or share your background location at any other time. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the app remains usable, but you may need to enter delivery addresses manually.

Device and usage information

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • A unique device identifier and a push notification token (if you have notifications enabled)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Screens viewed and actions taken inside the app, used in aggregate to improve the product
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

Communications you send us

If you contact aidaly@dapperdriver.com or use any in-app support feature, we keep a record of the conversation so we can help you and improve our service.

3. How we use your information

  • To run your account — create and authenticate your login, save your preferences, send order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • To process and deliver orders — share your first name, delivery address, and items with the fulfilling boutique and the assigned DapperDriver, and route the delivery.
  • To process payments — through Stripe.
  • To communicate with you about the Service — transactional messages (order status, password resets) are sent regardless of marketing opt-ins.
  • For our weekly letter and product announcements — only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To improve the product — we analyze aggregated, de-identified usage to understand what works.
  • To prevent fraud and protect accounts — detect suspicious activity, verify identity for high-value orders, comply with our payment processors' anti-fraud requirements.
  • To comply with the law — respond to lawful requests from authorities, enforce our Terms.

What we do not do: we do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever. We do not share your data with advertisers or third-party data brokers. We do not use your data to build profiles for targeted advertising on other platforms.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with people and services that help us run DapperDriver. We require each of them to handle your data securely and use it only for the purpose we engage them for.

Boutique partners

The boutique fulfilling your order receives your first name, the items you ordered, and the delivery address. They do not receive your phone number, email, payment information, or any data unrelated to your order.

DapperDriver couriers

The DapperDriver assigned to your delivery receives your first name, the items being delivered (number of bags, fragility), the pickup boutique address, and your delivery address. They do not receive your phone number or email. After the delivery is complete, the driver no longer has access to your address or order in our system.

Driver information shown to you

While your delivery is in progress, you (the shopper) can see the assigned driver's name, vehicle and license plate, rating, and an approximate live distance to your address so you can identify your order and know when it's near. You do not receive the driver's phone number. If either party needs to communicate, DapperDriver support facilitates contact.

Legal authorities

We may disclose information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of DapperDriver, our users, or others. We will notify you when we can.

Business transfers

If DapperDriver is acquired or merges with another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance of any such transfer.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account information — while your account is open, plus 30 days after deletion (to allow account recovery).
  • Order records — seven (7) years, to comply with U.S. tax and accounting requirements.
  • Payment tokens — for as long as your card is on file; immediately deleted on removal.
  • Marketing list — until you unsubscribe; we then delete your email from our list within 30 days.
  • Support correspondence — three (3) years.
  • Anonymized analytics — indefinitely; this data cannot be linked back to you.

6. Your rights and choices

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and personal information (see Section 7).
  • Export a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time, with no effect on transactional messages.
  • Adjust device permissions for location, push notifications, and the camera in your iOS or Android system settings.

To exercise any of these rights, write to aidaly@dapperdriver.com. We will respond within thirty (30) days.

7. Account deletion

You can delete your DapperDriver account at any time:

  • In the app — Settings → Account → Delete Account. The deletion takes effect immediately.
  • By email — write to aidaly@dapperdriver.com from the email address on file. We will confirm the request and delete your account within thirty (30) days.

When you delete your account, we remove your personal information from our active systems within 30 days. Order records may be retained in anonymized or aggregated form to comply with the legal retention periods listed in Section 5.

8. California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a resident of California, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act:

  • The right to know what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties we have shared it with.
  • The right to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. DapperDriver does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • The right to limit the use of "sensitive personal information." We do not use sensitive personal information for purposes that would trigger this right.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising any of the above.

To exercise these rights, contact aidaly@dapperdriver.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. EU and UK residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you the following rights:

  • The right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

The legal bases on which we process your data:

  • Performance of a contract — when we process orders, deliver them, and run your account.
  • Legitimate interests — when we use anonymized data to improve the Service, prevent fraud, and secure our infrastructure.
  • Consent — for marketing communications, location access, and push notifications.
  • Legal obligation — to retain records and respond to lawful requests.

Our data controller is DapperDriver, Inc. To exercise GDPR rights, write to aidaly@dapperdriver.com.

10. Children's privacy

DapperDriver is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact aidaly@dapperdriver.com and we will delete it promptly.

You must be 18 or older to place orders. If you are between 13 and 17, you may use the Service only with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian.

11. Security

We protect your information using industry-standard practices: TLS encryption for all data in transit, encryption at rest in our databases, hashed passwords, scoped access for our team, and routine security review of our infrastructure providers. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; we work to minimize risk and respond promptly to any incident.

If we discover a security incident that materially affects your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate regulators as required by law.

12. International data transfers

DapperDriver is based in the United States, and our service providers are primarily based in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. For transfers from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (or equivalent UK transfer mechanisms) with our service providers.

13. Push notifications and cookies

Push notifications

The DapperDriver app may send push notifications about your orders (transactional) and, if you opt in, about new boutiques and weekly letters (marketing). You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's system settings. Disabling them does not affect the rest of the Service.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses only essential cookies necessary for site functions like the waitlist form. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels. We may use privacy-respecting, server-side analytics (such as Plausible) that do not place identifying cookies in your browser.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices, our service providers, or applicable law change. When we make material changes, we will post the updated Policy here, change the "Effective" date at the top, and notify you in the app or by email at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date means you accept the updated Policy.

15. Contact us

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, email aidaly@dapperdriver.com. We aim to respond to every privacy inquiry within five (5) business days, and to resolve formal requests within thirty (30) days.
